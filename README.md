# Claire's Turnaround: A Senior Role Application Case Study

This repository contains the full application package I prepared for a Chief Technology Officer / Chief Information Officer position at Claire's (Ames Watson) in April 2026, following Claire's Chapter 11 filing in August 2025. The package was assembled as a complete transformation thesis with a defined playbook, named decisions, dated milestones, quantified outcomes, and a stated probability of success.

The role did not result in an offer. The work, however, stands on its own as an example of the depth of preparation and strategic thinking I bring to senior consulting and leadership opportunities. I keep it published here as a portfolio case study because the analysis, the financial modeling, and the operational planning are reusable demonstrations of capability that apply to any retail, multi-unit, or operational turnaround context.

## The thesis

Claire's filed Chapter 11 in August 2025 inside a growing market. The global specialty jewelry market is moving from $365.9B in 2024 to $580.7B by 2033 at 5-8% CAGR, with North America compounding at 8.4%. Claire's revenue is down 15-20% year over year across 2025 and 1,326 US stores are at risk. The package opens with a one-sentence framing that holds the entire argument together:

> This is execution failure, not market failure, and execution failure is fixable.

What follows in the materials is the analysis behind that claim and the eighteen-month operational program that would prove it.

### Adaptive resume and cover letter strategy

A central premise of the package is that different stakeholders care about different things. A board member reads for strategic clarity. A CFO reads for financial discipline. A peer technical executive reads for implementation credibility. Treating the application as a single static resume forecloses on stakeholders who happen to read first. The package therefore contains five tailored variants for each of two artifacts:

**Cover letters** (each dated 2026-04-29):

- [Custom](CoverLetter_Claires_Custom_2026-04-29.html) (opened to the specific Claire's situation)
- [Default](CoverLetter_Claires_Default_2026-04-29.html) (general positioning)
- [Full CV](CoverLetter_Claires_FullCV_2026-04-29.html) (comprehensive history)
- [Projects](CoverLetter_Claires_Projects_2026-04-29.html) (project-led narrative)
- [Skills First](CoverLetter_Claires_SkillsFirst_2026-04-29.html) (capability-led)

**Resumes** with matching variants:

- [Custom](Resume_Claires_Custom_2026-04-29.html), [Default](Resume_Claires_Default_2026-04-29.html), [Full CV](Resume_Claires_FullCV_2026-04-29.html), [Projects](Resume_Claires_Projects_2026-04-29.html), [Skills First](Resume_Claires_SkillsFirst_2026-04-29.html)

## What this package demonstrates

For someone evaluating me for a senior consulting engagement or a technology leadership role, this repository is intended as a working example of:

- **Strategic intelligence work**: external market sizing, competitive analysis, financial modeling, and root-cause diagnosis at a level appropriate for board-level decision making.
- **Operational planning**: a 90-day tactical plan with day-level granularity, not a slide of bullet points labeled "phase one."
- **Applied AI thinking**: a decision-making AI implementation guide that distinguishes operational AI (buying, allocation, forecasting under merchant governance) from chatbot AI, and that lays out the governance, data, and learning loops needed to make it work.
- **Multi-stakeholder framing**: the same underlying analysis presented through five distinct cover letter and resume lenses, so that whichever stakeholder reads first gets a coherent and tailored entry point.
- **Honest probabilistic communication**: a stated 60-70% success probability with named risks (organizational change, data migration complexity, holiday-season cutover, market downturn) and the management approach for each.
